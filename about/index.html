03 — Our methodology

TEAM — the Territorial Exposure Assessment Method

TEAM is our proprietary, in-house methodology. It reads a facility through the territory it depends on, and translates that exposure into a scored recommendation the organisation can defend: what to fund, what to defer, and what to act on now.

  1. 01

    Map

    We build the territorial picture of the operation: the facility, its providers and warehouses, the transport lines and client delivery. Official hazard and physical climate information is overlaid on that whole network, not only on the plot.

  2. 02

    Score

    A structured workshop with Operations together with Risk or Strategy weighs how critical each dependency is. TEAM then scores every dependency by what its failure would mean for the operation.

  3. 03

    Recommend

    Each material dependency is assigned one of four management postures, matched to the nature of the business — a graded call on downtime, capital and continuity.

What TEAM reads

  • Services
  • Roads
  • Access
  • Energy
  • Water
  • Logistics
  • Suppliers
  • Infrastructure
  • Workforce
  • Clients

How each dependency is scored

Disrupt
If it fails, the operation stops.
Slow
If it fails, the operation continues at reduced capacity or with delays.
Limited impact
If it fails, the effect on the operation is manageable.

Four management postures

Monitor
Keep the exposure in view and respond after an event.
Plan
Address it in a later investment cycle.
Prioritise
Prepare and schedule action, including lined-up alternatives.
Act immediately
Single-point or unacceptable exposure. Act now.

What the organisation receives

  • Territorial dependency maps — the picture you can brief.
  • TEAM scoring — the ranking you can defend.
  • Exposure categorisation and action — the call you can take.
