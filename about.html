About

Mission

We assist businesses and organisations to prepare for or respond to territorial disruption to ensure operational continuity and fast strategic recovery.

We turn territorial intelligence into clear decisions — from assessing exposure and protecting operational continuity to guiding strategic recovery.

Vision

Territorial strategy

Terranova Strategy is a territorial strategy consultancy. We help businesses and public organisations understand territorial exposure, navigate disruption, build resilience, recover after major disruption, and support long-term territorial development.

These fields sit in one reading of place — not as separate products. Climate resilience, territorial and physical climate risk, operational continuity, supply chain and logistics, strategic recovery, reconstruction, territorial and regional development, and sustainability are connected areas of the company’s mission and future development.

  1. Territorial strategy The overall frame: how a place, a network and an operation fit together.
  2. Climate resilience How physical climate risk and other territorial hazards condition what can continue to operate.
  3. Risk and exposure Territorial risk read through dependencies — infrastructure, access, energy, suppliers — not only the site.
  4. Operational continuity Business continuity and operational resilience once the territory around the operation is in view.
  5. Supply chain and logistics The corridors, warehouses and providers that can stop an operation even when the facility is intact.
  6. Strategic recovery After disruption: reconstruction sequenced for the territory, and a path toward territorial and regional development.

How we work today

The current commercial offer is three services. They cover before disruption, during the event, and after emergency works — the same territorial reading, at different moments.

Territorial Exposure Assessment

Before disruption: map territorial dependencies, score exposure, and decide what to fund, defer or act on.

Operational Disruption Response

During the event: time-to-reactivation, dual government and company tracks, and where capital should move now.

Strategic Territorial Recovery

After emergency works: reconstruction sequenced for the permanent territorial reset.

We assess the hazards, dependencies and territorial conditions that can affect your operations — giving you the clarity to navigate disruption and take strategic decisions.
