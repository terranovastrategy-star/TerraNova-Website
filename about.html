About

Territorial exposure, disruption and recovery strategy for businesses and public organisations.

Mission

We assist businesses and organisations to prevent or react in front of territorial disruption to ensure operational continuity and fast strategic recovery.

We turn territorial intelligence into clear decisions — from assessing exposure and protecting operational continuity to guiding strategic recovery.

Vision

Understand your territorial exposure before disruption occurs.

From anticipating exposure to navigating disruption and shaping recovery, we help organisations make clear territorial decisions at every stage.

Values

Territory first

We start with the systems and conditions surrounding the operation, not only the asset itself.

Business connected

Exposure is translated into operational and strategic consequences.

Portfolio oriented

Where relevant, we compare multiple locations to determine where attention should come first.

Decision ready

The objective is not to produce information. It is to enable a better decision.

We assess the hazards, dependencies and territorial conditions that can affect your operations — giving you the clarity to navigate disruption and take strategic decisions.
